# Outdated Apple Ads advice

Older guides, case studies and even older talks by the playbook's source still circulate.
If you find one of these, do not follow it.

| Old advice | Why it is outdated | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|
| Split every keyword into "new users", "returning users" and "open" ad groups | Targeting reaches only users with personalized ads on; Apple's user-type labels leak | One ad group per intent, no user-type targeting |
| Limit Ad Tracking (LAT) on/off ad groups, the "18+" trick | LAT no longer exists; AdServices attributes every user | Nothing to replace |
| Extrapolate untracked installs ("strict vs extended ROAS") | Attribution is now complete; view-through inflates rather than hides | Tap-through only |
| Creative Sets for screenshot tests, tests on screens 7–10 | Replaced by custom product pages; screens 7+ are rarely seen | CPPs per intent; design screens 1–6 |
| Search tab on CPM bidding | All placements are cost-per-tap now | Search results placement for performance |
| "Today tab is under-used reach" | Direct returns are poor; buyers are very large brands | Skip it on a performance budget |
| "Two-thirds of downloads come from search" | Mostly brand searches for big apps; Apple dropped the claim in 2026 | Size your own generic search demand |
| "Ads boost your organic rank" | No useful lift observed over years of heavy spend | Fix rank through ASO and ratings |
| Search Popularity as a volume figure | Unreliable since late 2025 | Impressions ÷ impression share for served terms |
| "Apple Search Ads" / API v5 | Renamed Apple Ads (2025); Campaign Management API v5 retires 2027-01-26 | Apple Ads Platform API v1 |
| High CPT + tight CPA cap everywhere | Cuts volume without better results on stable ad groups | CPA cap on Discovery only |
| Second-price auction | Apple Ads does not charge the second-highest bid | Test lower bids where share is high |
