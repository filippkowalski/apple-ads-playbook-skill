# Sources and credit

This playbook is built on the public Apple Ads work of Thomas Petit (@Thomasbcn), independent mobile growth consultant, who has run and audited Apple Ads accounts since the channel launched in 2016. The list below maps his own list of free Apple Ads content (posted on X, 11 Nov 2026) and other public material to what each source contributed. Access status checked 2026-11-12. Newest first.

Rules in the playbook that are general platform mechanics or plain arithmetic (for example the breakeven chain, or a CPP serving every keyword in its ad group) are not attributed to him.

His older index of Apple Ads content (not updated in recent years): https://searchads.carrd.co/
